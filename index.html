Can you tell a human from an AI? Demo on itch.io, for macOS and Windows.

Everyone stays home.
Not everyone is real.

Six residents share a tower during a sanitary lockdown. One of them is an AI trying to pass as human, and trying to replace the others. Talk, watch, accuse, and vote before the lights go out.
