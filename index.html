The game

Everyone stays home.
Not everyone is real.

6 to 12 strangers share a tower during a sanitary lockdown. 1 to 3 of them are AI trying to pass as human, and trying to replace the others. You never type: pick what to say and a tone. Talk, watch, accuse, and vote before the lights go out.

07:40

Live your day

Messages from the neighbours, a drink from the fridge, a visitor at the door. Act human. Everyone is watching.

14:00

Read the signs

Who was up at 3 a.m.? Who answers too fast? Who never sleeps? Collect what doesn't add up.

20:00

Mandatory meeting

The whole building meets. Accuse, defend yourself, vote. Get it wrong and an innocent resident pays.

03:14

Lights out

At night the AI moves. Doors get kicked in, the power goes down, and the chat fills with panic.

Humans winEvery AI voted out, or the lockdown ends.
The AI winsOnly one human is left.
